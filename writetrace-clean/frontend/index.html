Review Pipeline

The backend keeps the scoring engine, but the front end now presents it as a workflow rather than a single editor page.

Start from student side
1. CreateTeacher posts assignments from the dashboard.
2. DraftStudent writes within the selected assignment.
3. SubmitDraft and writing log are sent to the teacher queue.
4. ReviewTeacher inspects scores, paste evidence, and flagged sections.

This structure is designed to feel like a small academic product, not a demo page. It keeps the app focused on role-based workflows and manual review.
